How to import IMDb list into Movier App

#TOS#private

Step 1.Go to imdb.com, Select Your List. Step 2.Choose the list you want to import into Movier Step 3.If you have a big list, you have to click the icon at the edgest on the view Step 4.Drag down to the bottom of the page and click ‘Export this list’ Then the browser will start downloading your list which should be called xxxxx.csv. After doing this, you can simply click the Import IMDb list in the Movier App and follow the instructions. Hope you enjoy using the app.
